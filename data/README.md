# Your corpus goes here

`.gitignore` excludes everything in this directory except this file. That is deliberate:
your corpus is large, it is often not yours to redistribute, and nobody needs a copy of
it to read your code.

A06 wants at least 300 chunks of text you actually care about reading results from: your
own notes, a repository's docstrings, course transcripts. Save it as `data/corpus.txt`.

Three hundred **chunks**, not three hundred words.
